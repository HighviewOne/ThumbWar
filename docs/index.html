Features

Everything you need for a quick thumb wrestling fix.

🤖

VS Computer

Three AI difficulties: Easy, Medium, Hard. Each one cheats just a little more than the last.

👥

Two Players

Same device, landscape mode. One thumb each, split screen, no internet required.

🏆

Best of 3

Choose a single round or best-of-3 match before each game.

🎯

Pin Mechanics

Overlap your opponent and hold for 2.5 seconds. A ring fills from yellow to red as you close in on the win.

🔊

Sound & Haptics

Audio feedback and vibration on game events. Toggle either in settings.

📊

Stats Tracking

Wins, losses, and win streaks persisted across sessions.

How to Play

It's exactly like the schoolyard game.

Touch the screen

Drag your thumb to move it around the play area.

Chase your opponent

Move your thumb onto your opponent's thumb to start a pin.

Hold the pin

Keep contact — a progress ring fills from yellow to red.

Win the round

If the ring completes, you win! The pinned player can escape by dragging away.
